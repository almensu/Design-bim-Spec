# Flyer — Human View Example

## 1. Material Recognition
- Category: offline_marketing
- Type: flyer
- Mode: reference_reconstruction
- Primary intent: compact information + contact conversion

## 2. Design Intent
The headline establishes the offer, the service image explains the subject, supporting benefits provide proof, and the QR/contact block closes the path.

## 3. Visual Attention Path
Headline → hero visual → benefit cluster → QR/contact.

## 4. Layer Stack
Paper/background → decorative shapes → hero visual → text blocks → contact/QR.

## 5. Semantic Groups
Headline group, hero group, benefits group, contact group.

## 6. Spatial Layout
Preserve a clear top hook, central content body, and bottom contact region. Printed information needs stronger margins than edge-to-edge social creative.

## 7. Anchors and Constraints
Contact group remains attached to the bottom safe area. Benefit rows share alignment and equal vertical spacing.

## 8. Style System
High readability, restrained palette, clear typographic contrast, limited decorative density.

## 9. Occlusion and Cropping
Core text and QR stay fully inside safe area; decorative shapes may bleed.

## 10. Uncertainty / Needs Review
Unreadable small print must not be invented.

## 11. Reconstruction Notes
Preserve print legibility and information hierarchy before decorative fidelity.
