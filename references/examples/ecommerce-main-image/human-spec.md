# Ecommerce Main Image — Human View Example

## 1. Material Recognition
- Category: ecommerce_marketing
- Type: ecommerce_main_image
- Mode: design_planning
- Canvas: 1080 × 1350
- Aspect ratio: 4:5
- Primary intent: product recognition + conversion
- Confidence: 0.95

## 2. Design Intent
Make the product unmistakable first, communicate the core benefit second, then move attention into price and action.

## 3. Visual Attention Path
1. Product: dominant through scale, centrality, and isolation.
2. Headline: high-contrast large typography above the product.
3. Price: accent color and compact high-density grouping.
4. CTA: terminal action through enclosure and proximity to price.

## 4. Layer Stack
Background → decorative glow → product shadow → product → headline → price/badge → CTA.

## 5. Semantic Groups
- Hero group: product + glow + shadow.
- Headline group: eyebrow + headline + subtitle.
- Conversion group: price + promo badge + CTA.

## 6. Spatial Layout
The upper quarter carries the proposition; the central half is product-dominant; the lower quarter concentrates conversion information. Keep generous lateral safe margins.

## 7. Anchors and Constraints
- Headline group is anchored to safe-area top center.
- Product is centered on canvas and kept clear of headline.
- Price is below product.
- CTA is directly below price with a small fixed normalized gap.
- Promo badge attaches to the product's top-right region.

## 8. Style System
- Restrained cool background.
- One saturated accent reserved for conversion.
- Sans-serif hierarchy with large bold headline and compact secondary text.
- Soft product shadow; low decorative density.

## 9. Occlusion and Cropping
Product remains fully visible. Glow and shadow may extend behind it but must not obscure text.

## 10. Uncertainty / Needs Review
Exact brand font and final copy remain downstream inputs.

## 11. Reconstruction Notes
Preserve semantic grouping and attention order when adapting to another ratio; do not merely scale all coordinates uniformly.
